Why a companion app?

Some phones lock this down harder than others

A few phone brands aggressively restrict background call access to save battery. When that happens, the main app alone can't reliably capture the call — a small companion app closes that gap.

Built for strict OEMs

Phone brands with aggressive battery management sometimes need this extra step for calls to record reliably in the background.

Records nothing extra

The companion app only detects and captures the call itself, then hands it straight back to the main app. No analytics, no ads.

Completely optional

Most phones don't need it at all. Try the main app first — only install this if recordings aren't coming through reliably.

Setting up the Helper app

Four quick steps, done once.

1

Download the Helper APK

Tap the download button below. Your phone may warn you it's from outside the Play Store — that's expected for this companion app.

2

Install it

Open the downloaded file and allow installation when prompted. This is a one-time step per device.

3

Grant Phone & Microphone permissions

Open the Helper app and grant both permissions directly from its home screen.

4

Enable it in Accessibility settings

The Helper app guides you straight there and shows exactly which entry to turn on.

What this permission is for: the Helper app uses Android's Accessibility service purely to stay alive in the background on phones that would otherwise kill it, so it can detect and capture your calls reliably. It performs no other action and reads no screen content.
